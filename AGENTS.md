# Word Quest — AI assistant entry point

Applies to ChatGPT chat and any other AI assistant accessing this repository.

Read [docs/AI_COLLABORATION.md](docs/AI_COLLABORATION.md), [docs/Master_Blueprint.md](docs/Master_Blueprint.md), and the bilingual development guides before editing. Use the assigned GitHub **Issue** for task scope and handoff, a separate **feature branch + PR** for proposed changes, and `main` only as the accepted baseline. Different AI chats do not automatically share context.

The first joint pilot is [Issue #11](https://github.com/realnt/wordquest/issues/11). ChatGPT proposes scoped changes; Claude.ai independently reviews them; the owner decides whether to merge. Do not merge or change production without approval.

**Non-negotiable:** six units, legacy saved progress/localStorage keys, no replay XP inflation, English-first optional Chinese, accurate etymology, and device-specific voice caveats. No export/import of progress or cross-device sync in v1.5.x.
